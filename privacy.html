Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

In short: Score Magic does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. Everything happens on your iPad.

What we collect

Nothing. Score Magic has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party services. The app does not use the network, so it has nowhere to send your information.

Your scores

Scores you import are copied into the app's private storage on your iPad, along with your settings for each score (such as which parts are visible, volume, tempo and position). This data stays on your device. It is removed when you delete a score or delete the app.

Microphone

The microphone is used only in the sing-along practice feature, and only after you grant permission. Audio is analysed on the iPad to compare your pitch with the score. The recording is kept only until you record again or leave the score, and it is never sent anywhere. You can withdraw permission at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone; the rest of the app keeps working.

Other permissions

Score Magic reads only the files you choose to open or import. It does not access your contacts, location, photos, camera or advertising identifier.

Children

Because Score Magic collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Third-party software

Score Magic includes the open-source Verovio engraving library and the GeneralUser GS sound font. They run inside the app, locally, and do not collect or transmit data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
