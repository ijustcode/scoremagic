Practice from the score, with every voice at your fingertips.

Score Magic is a sheet-music player for iPad. Open a MusicXML score, hear it played back, and choose exactly which parts you see and hear.

Show and hide voices

Focus on your own part, or see the full score. Parts marked hidden in the file start hidden and silent.

Mute, solo, mix

Set volume per part, mute the ones you don't need, or solo a single voice to learn it.

Play from anywhere

Start at any bar, loop a difficult passage, change the tempo, and step through notes with the arrow keys.

Sing along

Practice mode listens through the microphone and compares your pitch with the score, entirely on the iPad.

Works offline

No accounts, no sign-in and no internet connection needed. Your library lives on your device.

Remembers your place

Visibility, mute, solo, volume, tempo, zoom, loop and position are saved for each score.

Getting scores in

Score Magic opens .mxl and .musicxml files. Use Import in the library, open a file from Files, or send one with AirDrop or the share sheet. Most notation programs, including MuseScore, can export MusicXML.

Score Magic is a player, not an editor.
